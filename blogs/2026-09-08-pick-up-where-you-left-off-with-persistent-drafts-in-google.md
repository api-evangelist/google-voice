We are introducing persistent drafts in Google Chat. Unsent messages are now automatically saved so you can finish and send later, and are also synchronized across your devices, allowing you to start composing a message on one device and finish or send it from another. With this update, you can start typing a message and finish later, even if you close Chat or reboot your device.
