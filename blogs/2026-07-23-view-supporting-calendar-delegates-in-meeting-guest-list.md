When viewing a guest list in Google Calendar on the web, you will now see a new icon next to leaders who have a calendar delegate assisting them with scheduling support. Hovering over the icon will display the person’s information, allowing you to easily initiate a chat with them directly. This update helps users quickly identify and contact the appropriate scheduling support for a leader to streamline communication and event coordination.
