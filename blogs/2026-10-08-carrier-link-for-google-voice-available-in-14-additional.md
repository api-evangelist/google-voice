In June , we launched Carrier Link in 20 countries where Google Voice service was not otherwise available. Today, we are making Carrier Link available in an additional 14 countries (the US, Canada, and much of Europe). Previously, customers in these countries could purchase Google Voice with a first party calling plan provided by Google.
