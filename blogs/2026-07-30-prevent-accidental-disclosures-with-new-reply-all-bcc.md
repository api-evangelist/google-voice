To help protect your privacy and ensure intentional communication, we are introducing a new caution notification in Gmail. This feature is designed to prevent accidental information disclosure by alerting users when they are about to "Reply All" to a thread where they were originally BCC’ed. Previously, users might inadvertently reveal their presence on a confidential thread by hitting "Reply All" while BCC'ed.
