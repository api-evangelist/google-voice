You can now switch from watching a Google Meet live stream to participating with audio and video directly on Android. This feature is available for viewers who have been invited to both the live stream and the main meeting. While watching a live stream on Android, you can switch roles using the meeting title, the live streaming badge, or the info button in the overflow menu.
