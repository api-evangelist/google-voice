You can now protect your calendar from repeated calendar spam and unwanted invitations. When you block a user in Google Calendar, the current event is automatically removed and you no longer receive new calendar invitations from that person. In addition, when you block an individual in Calendar, they’re added to your account-wide blocklist , and interactions across all supported Google products are blocked.
