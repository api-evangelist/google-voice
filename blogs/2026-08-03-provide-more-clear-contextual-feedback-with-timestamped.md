We are introducing timestamped comments for videos in Google Drive on the web. Users can now anchor comments to a specific time mark in a video, making video collaboration more direct, precise, and contextual. When reviewing a video file, users can choose between adding a timestamped comment or a general file-level comment using a split button in the commenting interface.
