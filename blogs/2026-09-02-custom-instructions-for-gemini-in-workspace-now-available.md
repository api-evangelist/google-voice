Earlier this year, we introduced the ability for Workspace users to set persistent custom instructions for Gemini in Google Docs . We're now expanding support for these custom instructions to additional Gemini in Workspace surfaces, specifically: Ask Gemini in Drive Ask Gemini in Chat Gemini side panel in Slides, Sheets, and Gmail These instructions help personalize your interactions with Gemini and ensure that Gemini adapts to your style, tone, and formatting preferences without needing to repeat them in every conversation, ultimately saving you time and ensuring consistency.
