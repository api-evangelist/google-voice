In April 2026, we updated Meet to improve video quality on high-resolution displays . We’re now updating the way the Admin console setting that limits video bandwidth works to reduce data usage and improve call quality. Previously, the ‘Limit video bandwidth’ setting only limited video bandwidth on the uplink; it now limits bandwidth on the downlink as well.
