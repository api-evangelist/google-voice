Admins can now access Google Chat usage metrics in the Gemini reports dashboard across both organization- and user-level reports. At the organization level, this allows admins to track active Gemini users in Google Chat, analyze usage trends, and identify who benefits most from Gemini-powered summarization and generation features. At the user level, admins can now view the level of Gemini adoption for specific users who actively engage with the app.
