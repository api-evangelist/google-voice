We are introducing the ability to use Gemini Notebook as a context source in Google Docs. Building on our Workspace Intelligence foundation, this feature bridges the gap between deep research and content creation by grounding your drafts in a curated knowledge base – all without the need to switch tabs or copy-paste between tools. If you’ve organized research in Gemini Notebook, you can now seamlessly tap into that library right where you write in Google Docs.
