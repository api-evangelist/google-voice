Trello for Google Workspace makes it easy to capture tasks and ideas from the conversations where they begin. With the app, you can create new Trello cards from emails and Google Chat messages, helping you keep Trello up to date without switching tabs or breaking your flow. The refreshed app replaces and builds on the Trello for Gmail experience and adds the ability to create new cards from Google Chat.
